# Third-party brand logos

The SVG files in `svg/` and the data in `src/brands.generated.ts` describe logos and marks that belong to other companies and projects. They are **not** part of Rune Icons, and the Apache License 2.0 in `LICENSE` covers only this package's code (`src/`, `scripts/`, `test/`), not the logos.

- Every logo is a trademark of its owner, included only so developers can identify the brand. Rune Icons is not affiliated with, sponsored by, or endorsed by any of them.
- Each logo keeps the license it was published under. It is in the `license` field, and `tier` groups them: `permissive`, `attribution` (credit the author), `share-alike`, and `trademark-only` (the brand's own guidelines apply).
- A `license` of CC0 or MIT describes the artwork file, not the trademark. You still need to follow the brand's guidelines, which are linked in `guidelines` (or `site`) where we have them.
- Some single-color logos are tinted to the brand's color. Do not otherwise alter, recolor, or animate a logo unless its guidelines allow it, and do not use one to suggest the brand endorses you.
- Logos under non-commercial, no-derivatives, or GPL licenses were left out.

If you own a logo in this package and want it removed or corrected, open an issue at https://github.com/Runeicons/runeicons/issues.
