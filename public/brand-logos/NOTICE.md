# Third-party brand logos

The SVG files in this folder are logos and marks owned by their respective companies and projects. They are shown only to identify the brand. They are not part of Rune Icons and are not covered by the Apache License 2.0 in the repository LICENSE. Rune Icons is not affiliated with or endorsed by any of them.

- The license each logo was published under is listed in `constants/brand-logos.ts`.
- Several licenses carry conditions: CC-BY needs credit to the author, CC-BY-SA is share-alike, and `brand-use`, `Trademark` and `Fair Use` mean the brand's own guidelines apply.
- Some single-color logos were tinted to the brand's color. Do not otherwise modify these logos.
- For official artwork and usage rules, use the brand's own website, also listed in `constants/brand-logos.ts`.
